# ADR 20261115051052164 - Sensors check in every 3 s and one 10 s offline cutoff applies to every board

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-15
- Source: feature/42#decision-2
- Scope: system-wide

The sensor library checks in every 3 s by default (still settable per board in its config file), and the service shows any sensor Offline after 10 s without a check-in, the same cutoff for every board (human-decided). At the old 10 s interval the room waited up to 10 s for a sound-list change to reach a board, up to 30 s for a dead board to show Offline and up to 10 s for a board to notice a calibration run, which is too slow on stage.

## Alternatives considered

A per-board cutoff that follows each board's own interval was rejected: every board moves to 3 s instead, and online stays one rule on the service clock (web/src/sensors/AGENTS.md).

## Consequences

- Boards on a library before 0.4.0 default to 10 s and flicker Offline under the 10 s cutoff; until they upgrade, their config file needs `checkin_interval = 3`, as the sensor contract, the library's quick start and the presenter checklist say. A board configured to check in less often than about every 10 s always looks offline.
- The Worker gets about three times as many check-ins.
- Everything that waits on a board's next check-in moved with the interval: the shared-id warning uses the same 10 s window, and calibration's no-audio timing is in web/src/AGENTS.md. Lengthening the interval again means a library release on every deployed board and a contract change, and moves those timings back.
