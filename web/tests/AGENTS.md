# Journey harness invariants

- A journey that changes the sound list (switch, teach, reteach, delete) or clears events never runs on the shared service: the other journeys compare its sound list with the seed and read its events. It starts its own service on its own port pair, allocated only in `ports.ts` (the base+50 block is full; the next such journey needs a new block, there and in the readiness doc's Ports line).
- Two-way ranging is exact only when each buzzer sits square to the pair line; a buzzer turned toward or away from the other board makes the distance short or long by up to its offset. A calibration journey keeps buzzers square (`Board.emitter_ccw_deg`) or its offsets small against its tolerance (the triangle journey: 2 cm against 10 cm).
- The live calibration rehearsal refuses its first step while any other calibration-capable board is online or the plan is a measured layout, and never clears events (agent-made): a run takes the first 3 capable sensors in plan order, and removing the rehearsal boards clears a measured layout the service cannot restore.
- The Devices panel opens over the sound list and holds two tabs (Devices, Event log), one shown at a time: a journey reaches a sensor's card, Calibrate, the sensitivity, the log or Clear events through `showDevices` or `showEventLog` (`fixtures/dashboard.ts`), and one that needs the sound list's switches together with the panel keeps the panel open in a second tab, as the sounds journey does. A click on a switch under the open panel waits until the test times out.
- A sensor library replay can stop right after fetching a new sound list, before the check-in that reports it (timing, not a rule). A journey that needs the service to see a sensor's new version replays it twice with the same state folder; a single replay makes that step flaky.
- A journey's own service (`SecondService.start`, `fixtures/service.ts`) counts as started once anything answers on its app port, so a foreign server already on the pair (another checkout's, a leftover) is used silently and the journey fails late on wrong data instead of at start. Before trusting a red journey that runs its own service, confirm its port pair was free.
